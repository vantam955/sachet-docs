Getting started with Sachet

Sachet is built for wellness brands with a small, focused lineup that customers reorder on a schedule. This guide takes you from installation to a store that matches the demo.

1. Install and publish

  1. In the Shopify Theme Store, open Sachet and click Try theme or Buy theme.
  2. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes. The theme appears in your theme library.
  3. Click Customize to open the theme editor, make your changes, then click Publish when you are ready.

The demo store's layout, colors, and typography come with the theme. Demo images and products do not.

2. Set up your products

Sachet works best with 2–10 products. For each product:

  • Add a product color (optional). Create a product metafield with the standard definition Color (custom.label_color). Product cards use it automatically. On the product page, connect the Product section's Label color setting to the metafield (see Product page). Leave it empty to use the theme accent color.
  • Add selling plans (optional). Install a subscriptions app such as Shopify Subscriptions and create plans such as delivery every month with a discount. Plan rows appear inside the product label automatically. When a plan name includes an interval (for example "Deliver every 2 weeks"), the product page also shows the Delivery timeline for the next 90 days (change it with the block's Time span setting).
  • Add days of supply (optional). Create a product metafield with the Integer type (custom.days_of_supply) and enter how many days one unit lasts. Cards, the product label, and the cart show it automatically (see Product page).
  • Add label rows. In the theme editor, open the product page, select the Label panel block, and add rows such as "Serving size: 2 capsules" or "Servings per container: 30". These rows are shared by all products; to give each product its own rows, use the custom.label_rows metafield (see Product page).

3. Choose your colors and fonts

Go to Theme settings:

  • Colors. Edit the palette grid to set your brand colors once. The seven theme colors below the grid reference the palette, so changing a palette color updates the whole store. Any section can override its background and text in Section colors; leave them empty to inherit the theme colors.
  • Typography. Pick a heading font and a body font from the font picker. Headings use a condensed font by default and can be set to uppercase or as entered. Body text size scales the whole layout proportionally (16px = default): headings, spacing, and page width follow, so the design keeps its balance.
  • Label style. Set the line thickness used for label borders and rules, and the corner radius.

4. Build your home page

Add sections from the section picker. The demo uses this order:

  1. Label banner — your headline, a short description, two buttons, and a few spec rows.
  2. Product lineup — every product as a numbered label row with its price and plan savings.
  3. Delivery steps — how subscriptions work, in three steps.
  4. Featured collection, Image with text, Blog posts, Email signup.

The Testimonials section is not on the default home page. Add it when you have real customer quotes you are allowed to use.

Each section has a Section colors group. Use it to alternate light and tinted bands down the page. The default home page already does this with your palette: Delivery steps and Blog posts on the tint color, and Email signup on the ink color with paper text.

5. Menus, header, and footer

  • Header. Upload a logo or keep your store name. Choose the Menu (Main menu by default) and the Customer account menu. Enable the sticky header if you want the header to stay visible while scrolling.
  • Announcement bar. Add a short message above the header.
  • Footer. Add blocks for menus, text, and email signup. Turn on payment icons, social media icons, Follow on Shop (requires the Shop sales channel), and the country and language selectors (shown only when your store sells in more than one country or language).

6. Before you launch

  • Add a favicon in Theme settings > Favicon (32 x 32px .png recommended).
  • Set up filters in the Search & Discovery app so customers can filter collections.
  • Add a contact page: create a page and choose the contact template in Theme template.
  • Preview the store on your phone. The theme is responsive, but check your images and copy.
