Header, announcement bar, and footer

Header

Open the theme editor and select Header in the header group.

SettingWhat it does
LogoUpload a logo. Landscape and portrait logos both work; the theme scales them to the width below. Without a logo, the store name is shown in the heading font.
Logo widthWidth of the logo in pixels on large screens.
MenuThe menu shown in the header. Default: Main menu. Up to three levels: the first level shows as a row on large screens and as a drawer on small screens. When the main menu does not fit on two lines, the header shows the menu button instead.
Customer account menuLinks in the account panel that opens from the account icon. Default: Customer account main menu.
Enable sticky headerKeeps the header visible while customers scroll.
Section colorsBackground and text for the header, drawer, and search box. Leave empty to use the theme colors.

The search icon opens a search box with suggestions as customers type. The cart icon opens the cart drawer or the cart page, depending on Theme settings > Cart > Cart type.

Customer accounts

The account icon is Shopify's account component. Customers can sign in without leaving the store. It appears when customer accounts are enabled in Settings > Customer accounts.

Announcement bar

A single line of text with an optional link, shown above the header. Add it from the header group with Add section. Use it for shipping notes or launch announcements.

Footer

The footer has blocks you can add, remove, and reorder (up to six):

  • Menu — a heading and a menu. The default footer has two: "Shop" with the Main menu and "Help" with the Footer menu. Pick any menu you created in Online Store > Navigation.
  • Text — a heading and rich text, for a short brand description or contact details.
  • Email signup — a heading, text, and the signup form. Subscribers are added to your customer list with email marketing consent.

Footer settings:

SettingWhat it does
Show social media iconsIcons for the accounts set in Theme settings > Social media. Only accounts with a link are shown.
Show Follow on ShopThe Follow on Shop button. Requires the Shop sales channel.
Show country/region selectorShown when your store sells in more than one country.
Show language selectorShown when your store has more than one language.
Show payment iconsIcons for the payment methods enabled on your store.
Section colorsThe demo footer uses the dark palette color as background.

The bottom bar shows the selectors, payment icons, copyright, and the "Powered by Shopify" link.
