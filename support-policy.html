Support policy

Buying Sachet includes support for the theme as published on the Shopify Theme Store.

What is included

  • Answers to questions about theme settings, sections, and features described in this documentation.
  • Fixes for bugs in the theme: broken layouts, dead links, logical errors, and features that do not work as documented.
  • Updates that keep the theme compatible with changes Shopify makes. You receive them through Online Store > Themes in your Shopify admin.

We reply to every support request within two business days (Monday to Friday). Critical issues that block checkout are handled first.

What is not included

  • Custom design or code changes to the theme.
  • Setting up, configuring, or troubleshooting third-party apps.
  • Migrating content from another theme or platform.
  • Help with edits you made to the theme code.

If you need any of these, we recommend hiring a Shopify Partner.

Editing the theme code

You can edit the theme code, but edited themes may no longer receive automatic updates, and we cannot support code you changed. Before editing:

  1. Go to Online Store > Themes, click the … menu next to your theme, and choose Duplicate.
  2. Edit the copy, not the published theme.
  3. Keep a note of the files you changed so you can reapply them after an update.

How to contact us

Use the contact form. Include your store URL (for example storename.myshopify.com), the page where the issue happens, and a screenshot if you can. You receive an automatic confirmation as soon as the form is submitted.

Need help? Contact us through the support form. We reply within two business days.
