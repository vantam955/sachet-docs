Collections, search, and cart

Collection page

Shows the collection title, description, and image, then the product grid. Settings: products per page, show collection description, show collection image, enable filtering, enable sorting, and section colors.

Product cards are "label fragments": a numbered band in the product color, the image on a tinted background, the title, the days of supply when set (see Product page), the price (with "From" when variants differ), the unit price when set, and Sale or Sold out badges.

Filters and sorting

Filters come from the Search & Discovery app: availability, price, product type, vendor, and variant options. Customers can combine filters; the active ones show as removable chips. The filter panel closes with the Escape key or when customers click outside it. Sorting uses Shopify's standard sort options.

Collection list page

All collections as cards, with pagination. Collections without an image use the first product's image.

Search

The search icon in the header opens a box that suggests products, articles, and pages as customers type. Pressing Enter opens the search page, which lists products, articles, and pages with pagination and a message when nothing matches.

Cart

Theme settings > Cart > Cart type chooses between:

  • Drawer — adding a product opens a panel over the current page. Customers can change quantities, remove items, add a note, and go to checkout without leaving the page.
  • Page — adding a product goes to the cart page.

Both show for each line: image, title, selected options, selling plan, days of supply, unit price, line discounts, and quantity. Below the lines: order discounts, subtotal, the tax note, the cart note (can be turned off), the checkout button, and dynamic checkout buttons.

If a customer tries to add more than the available stock, the cart shows Shopify's message and keeps the available quantity.
