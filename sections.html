Sections

Add sections to any page from the theme editor with Add section. Most sections have a heading, and every section has its own content settings and a Section colors group. Image size notes use the format recommended by Shopify.

Label banner

A large label with a caption, heading, text, two buttons, and spec rows. Blocks: Label row (name and value, for example "Ships every: 30 days"). Image: 1200 x 1200px .jpg recommended. Use it as the first section of the home page.

Product lineup

Every product in a collection as a numbered row with its price and, when it has selling plans, the best subscription saving. Choose the collection and the number of products to show.

Delivery steps

Up to six numbered steps (three by default) that explain how subscriptions work. Blocks: Step (heading and text).

Featured product

The full product label for one product, including purchase options and the add to cart button. Choosing a variant here does not change the page URL. Supports the same blocks as the product page and app blocks.

Featured collection

A grid of product cards from one collection. Leave the heading empty to use the collection name. Set the number of products and whether to show the "View all" link.

Collection list

Cards for several collections. Blocks: Collection. Collections without an image show the first product's image.

Image with text

An image next to a heading, text, and a button. Choose the image position. Image: 4:3 aspect ratio recommended.

Rich text

A heading, formatted text, and an optional button, for a short story or policy summary. Choose left or center alignment.

Email signup

A heading, text, and the signup form. Customers who sign up are added to your customer list with email marketing consent.

Video

A video uploaded to Shopify, or a YouTube or Vimeo link. Uploaded videos play instead of the link. Add a cover image and a video description for screen readers. Videos play when customers press play; they do not autoplay.

Blog posts

The latest articles from one blog, with image, title, and excerpt. Choose the blog and the number of articles.

Testimonials

Quotes from customers. Blocks: Testimonial (quote, author, author detail). Use real quotes with permission.

Logo list

Logos of press, certifications, or stockists. Blocks: Logo (image, name, optional link). Image: 320 x 140px .png recommended.

Custom Liquid

A box for your own Liquid or an app's snippet. Available on every page.

Product recommendations

Shown on product pages. Choose Related products (automatic) or Complementary products (set up in the Search & Discovery app). Hidden when there is nothing to show.

Contact form

Name, email, phone, and message fields. Used by the contact page template; can be added to any page.
