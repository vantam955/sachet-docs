Product page

The product page is a "label": every element is a block you can reorder, remove, or add in the theme editor. Open Online Store > Themes > Customize, choose Products > Default product, and select the Product section.

Product color

Each product can have its own color on the title band, its card on collection pages, and the background behind its images.

  1. In Settings > Custom data > Products, add a metafield with the Color type and the namespace and key custom.label_color.
  2. On each product, set the color.
  3. In the theme editor, select the Product section. Under Product label color, connect Label color to that metafield (click the dynamic source icon next to the setting).

Product cards read the metafield on their own; no connection is needed there.

Leave Label color empty to use the theme accent color. Text on label color sets the text on the band. Leave it empty and the theme picks a readable text color for each product: the theme Text or Background color, whichever contrasts more with the label color. Without a label color, the band uses the theme's Text on accent color.

Label rows for each product

The Label panel block's Rows are shared by every product that uses the template. To give a product its own rows, such as its serving size:

  1. In Settings > Custom data > Products, add a metafield with the Multi-line text type and the namespace and key custom.label_rows.

  2. On each product, enter one row per line, for example:

    Serving size: 1 scoop (6 g)
Servings per container: 30

Products with this metafield show their own rows; products without it show the block's Rows. The heading and the automatic "Lasts" row stay the same.

Days of supply

Tell customers how long one unit lasts, such as a 30-day supply of capsules.

  1. In Settings > Custom data > Products, add a metafield with the Integer type and the namespace and key custom.days_of_supply.
  2. On each product, enter the number of days one unit lasts, for example 30.

The theme then shows it in three places, with no editor setup:

  • Product cards: "30-day supply" below the title.
  • Product page: a "Lasts: 30 days" row at the end of the Label panel block, after your own rows.
  • Cart drawer and cart page: "30-day supply" below the product title, next to the selling plan name.

The number is per unit, so it does not change with the quantity in the cart. Leave the metafield empty to hide it.

Selling plans and subscriptions

Selling plans come from a subscriptions app, for example Shopify Subscriptions. After you create plans:

  • Each plan appears as a row in Purchase options with its price and discount note.
  • Plans that do not apply to the selected variant are hidden automatically.
  • The Delivery timeline reads the interval from the plan name. Name plans "Every 30 days" or "Deliver every 2 weeks" so the timeline can show the schedule. Names without an interval hide the timeline.

Recommendations

Two Product recommendations sections sit below the product by default: Complementary products and Related products. Related products are generated automatically. Complementary products are set up in the Search & Discovery app. A section stays hidden when it has nothing to show.

Media

The theme supports images, videos, 3D models, and YouTube or Vimeo videos. Images with different aspect ratios keep the same square frame. Variant images show when a customer selects the variant.
