Blog, pages, and other templates

Blog

The blog page lists articles with image, title, excerpt, author, and date, with tag filtering and pagination. Settings: articles per page, show tags, show author.

Article

Shows the title, publish date, featured image, content, and comments. Comments use Shopify's comment settings in Online Store > Blog posts > Manage comments: with moderation on, the theme tells the customer their comment is awaiting approval. Comments are paginated ten per page. Settings: show featured image, show author.

Pages

The Default page template shows the page title and content. The contact template (file page.contact.json) adds the contact form below the content. Assign a template in the page's Theme template setting in the Shopify admin.

404

Shows a "page not found" message, a search box, and links to the home page and all products.

Password page

Shown while your store is password protected. It uses your logo or store name, the password message from Online Store > Preferences, an email signup form, and the password form.

Gift card page

Sent to customers who buy a gift card: balance, code, QR code, an Apple Wallet button on iOS, and a link to print. The page uses your logo or store name.
