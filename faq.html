FAQ

Why don't subscription rows show on my product page?

The product has no selling plans. Selling plans are created by a subscriptions app such as Shopify Subscriptions. After you add a plan to the product, the rows appear in the Purchase options block.

Why is the delivery timeline hidden?

The theme reads the delivery interval from the selling plan name. Use a name that includes an interval, for example "Every 30 days" or "Deliver every 2 weeks". Names like "Subscribe and save" hide the timeline. The timeline also hides when the interval is longer than the block's Time span (90 days by default).

How do I change a product's color?

Set the custom.label_color metafield on the product. Product cards use it automatically. For the product page, select the Product section and connect its Label color setting to that metafield. See Product page.

Why does every product show the same label rows?

The Rows in the Label panel block belong to the template, so all products share them. Create the custom.label_rows metafield (Multi-line text type) and fill it in on each product; those rows replace the shared ones. See Product page.

How do I show how long a product lasts?

Create the custom.days_of_supply metafield (Integer type) and enter the number of days one unit lasts. Cards and the cart show "30-day supply", and the product label adds a "Lasts: 30 days" row. No theme setting is needed. See Product page.

How do I change the theme's colors?

Go to Theme settings > Colors. Edit the palette grid to change your brand colors everywhere. To give one section a different background, open that section and set Section colors. Clear them to go back to the theme colors.

Why are my section colors unreadable?

You set a background without a text color. The theme then picks a light or dark text automatically, but it cannot fix a background that is close to the text color you chose. Pick a text color with enough contrast, or clear both fields.

Why does my desktop header show a menu button instead of the menu?

The main menu does not fit on two lines at that screen width, so the header shows the menu button instead, as it does on phones. Shorten the top-level menu titles or move some links into submenus to show the menu as a row again.

Where are the country and language selectors?

They appear in the footer when your store sells in more than one country (Settings > Markets) or more than one language (Settings > Languages). With a single country and language, nothing is shown.

Why don't I see the Follow on Shop button?

It requires the Shop sales channel. Install it from the Shopify App Store, then turn on Show Follow on Shop in the footer settings.

How do I set up collection filters?

Install the free Search & Discovery app and choose which filters to show. The theme displays them on collection and search pages.

Can I add an app block to the product page?

Yes. In the theme editor, open the Product section, click Add block, and choose the app's block. App blocks also work in the Featured product section.

How do I translate the theme?

Use Settings > Languages and the Translate & Adapt app. All theme text is translatable, including section defaults.

How do I update the theme?

When a new version is available, you see a notice in Online Store > Themes. Updates keep your settings. If you edited the theme code, the update is added to your library as a new copy so you can move your changes.

Can I use a different font?

Yes. Theme settings > Typography uses Shopify's font library, including system fonts such as Helvetica and New York. Custom font files are not supported.

Why did everything get bigger when I changed the body text size?

Body text size scales the whole layout proportionally (16px = default): headings, spacing, and page width follow the body text, like zooming the page. Use 16px for the layout shown in the demo.
